01 Our Story

From the cedars of Lebanon to the shores of the Gambia.

For generations, Lebanese families have been part of West African life — trading, building, raising children, and always, sharing their table.

Al Jawad opens that table to everyone in The Gambia. We are a butchery and restaurant under one roof: we choose and cut our own meat, bake bread on the saj, and cook our skewers over glowing charcoal — slowly, the way it has always been done from Beirut to the villages of the south.

Our name, الجواد, speaks of generosity. You will taste it in every plate we send out.

02 What we bring

Four things The Gambia has never tasted like this.

I

The Butcher’s Counter

A true Lebanese butchery under one roof. Lamb, beef and chicken selected, cut and marinated in-house — so what reaches the grill is exactly what we chose.

II

Real Charcoal Mashawi

Kafta, tawook, kabab and lamb over live coals. Smoke, char and patience — the flavour gas grills can’t fake.

III

The Mezze Table

Hummus, moutabbal, warak enab, fattoush, kibbeh. Small plates for the middle of the table — Lebanon’s way of saying stay a while.

IV

Breakfast to Shisha

Foul and manakish in the morning, steaks and skewers at dinner, Lebanese coffee and shisha to linger over.
